Privacy Policy

DinoLife — Wandering Turtle Games
Last updated: June 10, 2026

Introduction

Wandering Turtle Games (“we”, “our”, or “us”) operates the DinoLife mobile application (the “App”). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our App.

Information We Collect

Information We Do Not Collect

DinoLife does not require account creation, login, or registration. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or location.

All game save data (progress, settings, and scores) is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to any server.

Information Collected by Third Parties

Our App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect certain information automatically, including:

  • Advertising identifiers (e.g., Google Advertising ID, IDFA)
  • Device information (e.g., device model, operating system version)
  • IP address (used for general location-based ad targeting, not stored by us)
  • Ad interaction data (e.g., clicks, views)

This data is collected and processed by Google in accordance with Google’s Privacy Policy. We do not have access to or control over the data collected by Google AdMob.

For more information on how Google uses data from apps that use their services, visit: Google Partner Sites Policy.

How We Use Information

We do not collect or use any personal information. The third-party advertising data collected by Google AdMob is used solely to serve relevant advertisements within the App.

Children’s Privacy

DinoLife does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. The App does not require any personal information to function. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

Data Retention

Since we do not collect personal data, there is no personal data retained on our servers. Game data stored on your device can be deleted by uninstalling the App.

Your Rights

You can reset your advertising identifier or opt out of personalized advertising through your device settings:

  • Android: Settings > Google > Ads > Reset advertising ID / Opt out of Ads Personalization
  • iOS: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the “Last updated” date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
